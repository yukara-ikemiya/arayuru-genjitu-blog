## 2. Sprinkle Bamboo Charcoal Powder

Keep these crucial points in mind:
* Lightly coat the meat surface with rendered cooking oil/fat before dusting.
* Do **not** over-apply charcoal.

Too much charcoal makes it aggressively black and gives an unpleasant gritty texture. Start with a light dusting.

### Control vs. Simulated Charcoal Grill (Moderate)

Surprisingly, the simulated charcoal grill tastes remarkably delicious.
Despite being a straightforward hack, the smoky flavor faithfully replicates authentic charcoal grilling (*sumibiyaki*).

### Moderate vs. Excessive Charcoal

The one with excessive charcoal looks like squid ink pasta. However, the texture feels distinctly gritty like eating actual embers. Moderation is key.

## Summary

We successfully simulated authentic charcoal grilling at home without needing a messy charcoal hearth. With a bit of creative thinking, domestic culinary "walls" can easily be overcome.

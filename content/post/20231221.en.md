**[Wall Advent Calendar 2023](https://adventar.org/calendars/9082) Day 21**

Whether preparing for a wedding, shaking off holiday pounds, or getting ready for summer, we often find ourselves wanting to lose around 3 kg (6-7 lbs) in a month.
However, because human discipline is inherently fragile, many people hit a stubborn "wall" when dieting.

Problem Statement: Lose 3–5 kg in 1 month  
Challenge: Dieting is painful.

## Proposed Method

Since restrictive diets are agonizing, let us consider a **"Diet where you MUST eat"**.
The concept is simple: your only constraint for the day is that you must finish a specific massive dish. Beyond that, you are free to eat whatever you want.

### Step 1: Cook a Massive Hotpot (*Nabe*)

At your first meal of the day, cook a hotpot with approximately 4 servings per person.
At this moment, do not ponder what you "feel like eating today." Leave emotions out of it.

For men, make a sacred vow to finish this entire pot within the day. For women, dividing it across two days works well.

To ensure sufficient protein intake, aim to include around 400g of meat or fish.
Vegetable calories are virtually negligible, so calorie intake is managed almost entirely by adjusting meat and fish amounts.

### Step 2: Eat

Whether you crave ramen or Italian food that day, first finish this entire pot of *nabe*.

Hotpot soup bases range from 100 kcal (light broth) to 500 kcal (rich broth). Even at the highest estimate, the entire pot totals around 1,400–1,600 kcal (~800 kcal for half).
After eating this massive volume of fiber and protein, your physical capacity to overeat other high-calorie junk is naturally eliminated.
